# Brand: Genus Coffee Box

A sub-brand of Genus Coffee (Oradea). It reuses what Genus already owns: matte black bags with a cream label, kraft paper, black linocut illustrations with a Romanian meander border, Space Grotesk, Instrument Sans and the Genus red. The box needs to look like Genus and still read as "the box", not "a bag".

## Positioning (from offer.md)

**Great Romanian coffee without the bother of buying it.** For home brewers who'd rather drink good coffee than shop for it, and for people giving a coffee gift. Core buyers: explorers and convenience buyers (50–56% bought in the panel), plus gift buyers.

## Promise and tagline

**Promise:** Every month, coffee roasted in the last 7 days, chosen for how you brew, at your locker, with nothing for you to do.

Tagline options:
1. **Cafea bună, fără bătăi de cap.** / Great coffee, no hassle. *(recommended)*
2. Romania's roasters, at your door.
3. You brew. We choose.

## Voice

| Adjective | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Easy** | Short sentences, one action per line, "we do it, you drink it". | Jargon walls, "extraction yield" on the homepage. |
| **Knowledgeable, not snobbish** | Explain why a coffee tastes like it does in one line; advanced notes on the back of the card. | "If you don't use a scale you're doing it wrong." |
| **Local and warm** | Name the roasters and their towns; write in Romanian first. | Generic "artisan", "premium", "world-class". |

**Before (pitch v1):** "Every month: a box with three 250g bags of specialty coffee from three different Romanian roasters (two guest roasters plus one Genus coffee), chosen around a theme."
**After:** "Three Romanian roasters, one box, every month. We pick to match how you brew, you rate what you drink, and next month's box gets better."

### Type

These are already on the Genus site and are free Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License):
- **Display:** Space Grotesk 500–700, for headings, box names and prices.
- **Text:** Instrument Sans 400–700, for body text and cards.
- **Data:** IBM Plex Mono 400–500, for roast dates, altitudes and ratings.

### Logo brief (box mark)

- **What it must say:** Genus, plus "box / every month / three roasters".
- **Concept:** the existing Genus wordmark above a small seal. The seal is a circle with the Romanian meander band, with "COFFEE BOX · OR ADEA" set on the arc. The tier number (2 or 3) sits in the centre.
- **Where it must work:**
  - a printed sticker seal on a black box (5cm)
  - a one-colour stamp on kraft
  - a 32px favicon (seal only, number in the centre)
  - an Instagram avatar
- **Avoid:**
  - coffee-bean or cup clichés
  - gradients
  - anything that needs colour to read; it must work in one colour
- These are concepts on the brand board, not a finished logo. A designer should draw the final seal.

### First five touchpoints

1. **Homepage `/box`:** headline "Cafea bună, fără bătăi de cap.", the three boxes as cards with their tier colour, and a quiz button as the only action.
2. **The box:** matte black mailer, sticker seal in Genus Red, kraft tissue inside, three bags in a row, a card for each bag, and a "how to rate" card with a QR code.
3. **Order confirmation email:** "Your first box: coffees, roast dates, and when it lands. Want to swap one? Tap here." Includes the date of the next preview email.
4. **First social post:** a top-down photo of the open box, with the three roasters tagged. Caption: "Trei prăjitorii, o cutie. December's box: …"
5. **Reply to the first complaint:** "Sorry, that shouldn't have happened. We've put 25 lei off your next box and we'll send [replacement] if you want it. What went wrong: the roast date, the taste, or the delivery?" (Within 24 hours, always signed with a name.)
