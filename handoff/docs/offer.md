# Offer: Genus Coffee Box

**Name:** Genus Coffee Box: "Great Romanian coffee without the bother of buying it."
**Built from:** the 100-buyer panel's objections (`panel/results.md`), the board's conditions (`board.md`) and the line-up in `pricing.md`. Method: the Offers lens (value equation).

## The stack

- **Core:** coffee that matches your taste arrives at your locker, fresh, without you choosing or ordering. Duo 159 / Trio 219 / Reserve 259 lei, monthly or every 2 months; any box once for 20 lei more.
- **Bonus 1: Preview & swap.** You see the coffees and roast dates 5 days ahead and can swap, skip or cancel in one tap. Kills "someone else picks" and "another subscription to cancel".
- **Bonus 2: Taste quiz and ratings.** The box learns your taste, and you can reorder favourites. Kills "coffees I won't like".
- **Bonus 3: Advanced brew notes** on every card. Kills "cards are for beginners".
- **Promise:** roast date on every bag, shipped within 7 days of roasting.
- **Guarantee:** rate any coffee 1–2★ and get 25 lei off your next box (one credit per box). At a 10% claim rate this costs about 2.5 lei per box, so Trio profit goes from 46 to about 43 lei. Wording needs checking against Romanian consumer law (ANPC). This is not legal advice.
- **Urgency (true only):** gift orders by 15 December arrive before Christmas. The first-box 199 lei price ends 31 January 2027.

## Re-test

The new `pitch.md` was shown to the first 10 buyers of the same deal (seed 331515). The old pitch is kept as `pitch-v1.md`. Results are in the section below.

## Re-test result (same 10 buyers, P001–P010 of the original panel)

**Before (v1 pitch, Trio 229 lei): 1 buy · 9 pass. After (this offer): 3 buy · 7 pass.** With 10 simulated buyers this is a direction, not proof.

| Buyer | Type | Before | After | What did it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P004 Hamza | gift buyer, convenience | pass | **buys Gift Trio** | one-off gift box sent to the recipient with a note |
| P007 Poppy | gift giver | buys gift | buys Gift Trio | (unchanged) |
| P008 Dana | gear nerd, high earner | pass | **buys first Trio at 199 lei** | printed roast dates, ships ≤7 days, preview & swap |
| P001, P002, P006, P010 | loyal to a roaster | pass | pass | want their own roaster in the box |
| P003, P005, P009 | price-conscious | pass | pass | want a one-off Duo at about 120–140 lei |

**Objections that dropped:** "I don't want a subscription" (gift buyers), "no roast date / stale" (gear nerd), "750g is too much" (nobody raised it once the Duo and every-2-months option existed).

**Objections still standing:**
1. **Price for the price-conscious:** they price a Duo at about 120 lei as a bargain and about 150–175 lei as expensive. Genus can't reach that with a guest bag at 45 lei. Don't chase them; they are not the customer.
2. **Loyal to their roaster:** "my own roaster in the box". A "nominate your roaster" form doubles as a list of roasters to recruit as partners.
3. **New ones:** a gift buyer worries the recipient gets pushed into a subscription (keep checkout for gifts subscription-free and say so). A purist wants shipping within 2 days of roasting, not 7. If partners can do it, tighten to "ships within 3 days of roasting".

**Change before launch:** add a trial box with no subscription, priced at the first-box offer, for example "First box 199 lei, no subscription needed". That answers the most repeated remaining request, a first-box discount without signing up, at no extra cost versus the subscription offer.
