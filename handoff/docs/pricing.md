# Pricing: Genus Marketplace

Positioning: **"Great coffee without the bother of buying it."** We pick the coffee, it arrives in your locker, you rate it, and we learn your taste.

## What buyers said (100 simulated buyers, see pricing-curve.md)

The acceptable range for a box is **130–250 lei**. As many buyers call a price a bargain as call it expensive at about **180 lei**. Median answers: 160 lei is a bargain, 220 lei is getting expensive, 260 lei is too expensive. These are simulated buyers, so the numbers tell us what to test, not what to charge.

## The line-up

Margins exclude 11% VAT (unconfirmed). Cost assumptions: guest bag 45 lei, Genus bag 25 lei, box 12 lei (gift box 22 lei), locker shipping 15 lei, packing 5 lei, Stripe fees.

| Box | What's in it | Subscription | One-off (no subscription) | Profit per box |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Duo** | 1 guest roaster + 1 Genus, 2 × 250g | 159 lei / month or every 2 months | 179 lei | 38 / 55 lei |
| **Trio** (core) | 2 guest roasters + 1 Genus, 3 × 250g | 219 lei / month | 239 lei | 46 / 64 lei |
| **Reserve** | Trio with one limited or micro-lot coffee | 259 lei / month | — | 66 lei |
| **Gift Trio** | Trio in a gift box with a handwritten note, sent to the recipient | — | 259 lei | 71 lei |

**Why each step up:**
- **Duo → Trio:** a third roaster for 60 lei more. For couples and anyone who drinks a V60 every day.
- **Trio → Reserve:** coffees you can't easily buy yourself. This is for explorers and enthusiasts, who said they'd pay 250–350 lei.
- **One-off → subscription:** the same box is 20 lei cheaper on a subscription, and you get the taste-matching and the preview-and-swap (below). A one-off is the easy first try and the gift. A subscription is the "never think about it again" option.

## How reviews drive what we send

1. **At sign-up:** brew method (filter, espresso or both), roast preference, and how much coffee you drink a month. That answer sets Duo or Trio and monthly or every 2 months.
2. **Every box:** a QR code on the card opens that box's review page. You rate each coffee 1–5 and tick flavour words. The aggregate ratings are public, and they become the "Genus ranking of Romanian roasters".
3. **We use the ratings:** next month's guest coffees are chosen against your profile. A 5-star coffee can be reordered as a full bag (add-on at the roaster's retail price), and a 1-star style isn't sent again.
4. **5 days before billing:** an email with the month's line-up and roast dates, with one tap to swap a coffee or skip the month. This answers the panel's top objections about losing control, freshness and forgetting to cancel.

## Opening offer

**The first Trio subscription box is 199 lei, for sign-ups until 31 January 2027.** Profit on that box is about 28 lei, which stays positive. It is a one-time first-box price, not a permanent discount, so it doesn't teach buyers to wait for sales. Christmas gift boxes are full price.

## What to test with real buyers

- **Trio at 219 vs 229 lei.** 229 lei adds about 9 lei per box. Test with two landing-page variants on the waitlist and a pre-sale at each price.
- **Duo at 149 vs 159 lei.** 149 lei leaves only about 29 lei per box. Keep 159 unless sign-ups at 159 are clearly weak.
- **Gift Trio at 249 vs 259 lei** during the Christmas pre-sale.

## The panel's top price objections, for marketing to answer

1. "That works out to about 76 lei a bag, more than I pay buying specialty bags on sale." (P003)
2. "750g a month is more coffee than I drink." (most common objection; the Duo and the every-2-months option answer it)
3. "It's one more subscription to remember to cancel." (answered by the preview email with one-tap skip)
